# Product

## Register

product

## Users

TinyMails is for solo founders, consultants, and small teams who already have permission to contact a modest list of people. They work from their own computer, use Gmail as the sending identity, and want to review every campaign before it leaves their account.

## Product Purpose

TinyMails turns a contact list and a Gmail account into a careful, resumable mail merge. It keeps data local, makes sending limits visible, prevents duplicate or suppressed sends, and preserves an audit trail. Success means a technical user can connect Gmail, import a list, send a test, start a capped campaign, and recover safely after a restart.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, candid, and capable. The product should feel like a small, well-made desktop utility: calm enough for review work, precise enough to trust with a real inbox, and friendly without becoming cute.

## Anti-references

- Growth-hacking dashboards that reward volume over judgment.
- Newsletter builders with decorative canvases, funnels, and vanity metrics.
- Generic AI SaaS surfaces with purple gradients, glass panels, oversized metrics, or decorative motion.
- Enterprise admin tools that bury the next action under dense navigation and configuration.

## Design Principles

- Make the next safe action obvious.
- Show exactly what will happen before Gmail is used.
- Keep risk, limits, and blocked recipients visible at the decision point.
- Use familiar controls and plain language so the interface disappears into the task.
- Keep local ownership legible: data location, account identity, and connection status are never hidden.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA for contrast, keyboard access, focus visibility, names, and status messaging. Never rely on color alone. Respect reduced-motion preferences, maintain readable type at browser zoom, and preserve useful workflows on narrow screens.
