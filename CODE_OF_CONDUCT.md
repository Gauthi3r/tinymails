# Code of conduct

TinyMails contributors should be direct, patient, and respectful.

We welcome people regardless of experience, identity, disability, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation. Harassment, threats, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, and sustained personal attacks are not acceptable.

Discuss the work. Critique code and decisions with specific evidence. Do not turn disagreement into judgment about a person. Respect requests to stop an interaction.

Repository maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior makes the project unsafe or unproductive. Report conduct problems through the repository's private maintainer contact. Maintainers should protect the reporter's privacy and respond in proportion to the behavior.
